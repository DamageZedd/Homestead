  ◈ 3-Tier Outpost Evolution
    • Tier 1 (Scout Camp): Basic frontier campfire with small garrison.
    • Tier 2 (Fortified Outpost): Adds sandbags, gas lamps, radios, and rare loot.
    • Tier 3 (Regional Stronghold): Heavily fortified garrison with display cases,
      safes, toolkits, and elite veteran reinforcements.

  ◈ Post-Emission Land Scramble
    Following Blowouts and Psi-Storms, factions deploy expeditions into the field
    to seize newly opened territory (65% bonus spawn chance).

  ◈ Fast Retaliation Raids
    Destroying an enemy stronghold triggers a 30% chance of a dedicated hit squad
    deployed to hunt down the attackers within 2 to 4 hours.

  ◈ Dynamic Supply Couriers
    Fortified camps dispatch 2-man supply couriers on foot across the map.
    Intercepting them yields rich ammunition and medical supply pouches.

  ◈ SOS Distress Signals
    Friendly outposts under siege broadcast emergency calls to your PDA.
    Rescuing the garrison awards cash rewards, faction goodwill, and stashes.

  ◈ Emission Zombification & Mutant Nests
    Unprotected garrisons caught in emissions turn into hostile zombified nests.
    Outposts overrun by mutants convert into active mutant dens marked on the PDA.

  ◈ Chest Interaction & Dismantling
    Loot rival camp chests or hold Shift+F to safely dismantle the container and
    transfer all contained loot directly into your personal inventory.
